Quick Reference Protocols
Anaphylaxis: Administer epinephrine (EpiPen), call ambulance, lay flat with legs elevated, monitor airway.
Seizure: Clear area, cushion head, turn sideways, do NOT restrain. Call nurse if >5 min.
Asthma: Sit upright, administer reliever inhaler, calm student, call emergency if no improvement in 5 min.
Diabetic Low: Give 15g fast-acting sugar (juice, glucose tabs), recheck in 15 min, call nurse.
